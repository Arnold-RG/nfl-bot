Privacy

Elvora privacy policy

Last updated 6 September 2026

This is the public policy for the Elvora wellness app. It is not a medical device and does not provide diagnosis or treatment. No private member data is published on this site.

Who we are

Elvora provides a health and fitness coaching app for wellness: activity, training, nutrition, and recovery.

Data we process

  • Account basics you enter (name, email, goals, body metrics)
  • Activity and fitness logs (steps, workouts, meals, water, sleep you log)
  • Optional device permissions: camera (meal photos), microphone (voice coach), activity recognition, Bluetooth (watch pairing), biometrics (optional lock)
  • Optional Health Connect / wearable readings when you connect them
  • Technical diagnostics needed to run and improve the app

How we use data

To provide coaching, personalize plans, sync across your devices, process subscriptions, and improve reliability. Elvora is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal health data. We may use processors (hosting, model APIs, analytics, payment providers) under contracts that limit use to providing the service.

Retention and your controls

You can export or request deletion of account data from in-app privacy settings or by contacting us. Some data may remain in backups for a limited period as required by law or security.

Children

Elvora is not directed at children under 13, or under the age required in your country.

Contact

Privacy questions: open an issue on the Elvora repository. A dedicated contact address has not been published yet.
