New coach mark

Not a cartoon — a live signal.

The coach identity is a geometric mark: ring, bronze core, and voice arcs. It reacts when Bot listens, thinks, and speaks — same symbol in the app and on this site.

  • Listening — soft arcs
  • Thinking — rotating ring
  • Speaking — stronger pulse
