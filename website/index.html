Four pillars

Built as a platform, not a calorie counter

Fitness intelligence

Adaptive workouts, progressive overload, GPS activity, and form demos that match your equipment and recovery.

Smart nutrition

Photo, voice, and text logging with confirmation. Macros, micronutrients, meal plans, and regional foods.

Personal AI coach

Voice and chat that understand your logs. Plans you can edit. Memory you can inspect and delete.

Health insights

Readiness, sleep, hydration, streaks, and progress — with a clear split between measured data and estimates.

Five destinations. One connected day.

  1. Home

    Daily command center

    Readiness, calories versus effort, macros, water, steps, sleep, and your next best action.

  2. Train

    Intelligent fitness

    Plans for gym, home, or outdoors. Logging, substitutions, and GPS walks or runs.

  3. Nutrition

    Food intelligence

    Diary, plate scan, recipes, hydration, and meal suggestions from remaining targets.

  4. Coach

    Voice assistant

    Wake Elvora, ask about your day, and get guidance that cites your own records.

  5. Progress

    Personal analytics

    Measurements, photos, weekly recaps, and exports. Privacy first.
