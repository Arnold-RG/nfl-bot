Why this exists

Most fitness apps show you a perfect week you never lived.

NFL BOT does the opposite. Open it fresh and the diary is blank, the streak is zero, and Bot has nothing clever to say about calories you didn’t eat. That is not a bug — it is the product rule: if a number is on screen, you put it there (or your watch / phone sensors did).

Bot is a cartoon coach with a moving head and mouth. You wake him with “hey bot”, ask about food, training, water, sleep, or the walk you just tracked on the map. He answers from your logs. Wellness guidance only — not a doctor.

How it works — full walkthrough

From first open to a real coaching loop

Follow the steps below. Each one shows what you see on the phone and what happens behind it.

Step 1

Open Today

The home tab greets you by name. In the center sits Bot — floating, blinking, waiting. Under him you get a Bot briefing built only from what already exists today (steps, water, meals, streak). If nothing exists yet, the copy says so plainly.

  • Quick vitals: Water, Steps, Sleep — tap to open each tool
  • Next best move: scan a meal or open a form demo
  • Bottom bar: Today · Diary · Add · Bot · You
Step 2

Wake Bot with “hey bot”

Open the Bot tab (or the live stage). Bot fills most of the screen. Say the wake phrase out loud, or tap Talk. While Bot speaks, the mouth opens and closes; while he thinks, the head turns; while he listens, he leans in.

  • Hands-free mode: mic reopens after each answer
  • Mute voice anytime in Account → Coach
  • Quiet hours (optional): Bot won’t initiate after 21:00
Step 3

Scan a meal (Add / Diary)

Tap Add or Scan meal. Take a photo of your plate. The vision pipeline suggests foods and macros. Nothing is saved until you confirm. That confirmed meal becomes Diary fuel — and Bot can finally talk about your day with real numbers.

  • Camera permission asked in context
  • No sample meals on a fresh install
  • Confirm screen is the gate to your calorie total
Step 4

Water, map, sleep

From Today or You: log water for the day and for the workout session, start a GPS track on the map (route line + km + steps), and log sleep hours/quality. Each of these feeds readiness and the daily briefing.

  • Water chips: +250 ml, +500 ml, workout sips
  • Map: OpenStreetMap tiles, pin follows you
  • Sleep sheet: hours + quality slider
Step 5

Train with human form demos

Open Form demos (You or Train). You get a black stage with a human figure, blue glow on working muscles, a carousel of moves, set dots, and a LOG SET button — same pattern as a serious training coach app, not a vague GIF library.

  • Example: Sprinter Lunge — 4×10 each side
  • Logging a set advances the session
  • Workout water resets when the session ends
Step 6

You — readiness, mood, reminders, recap

The You hub is where the quieter tools live: readiness score (sleep + water + steps), mood check-in, reminders, progress photo notes, weekly calorie bars, and a rebuilt Account screen (profile, coach voice toggles, goals, watch, permissions).

  • Readiness is honest when inputs are missing
  • Mood changes Bot’s tone, not a diagnosis
  • Account was rebuilt from scratch — cleaner, fewer cluttered settings

The loop, in one sentence

Wake Bot → log something real (meal, water, walk, set, sleep, mood) → Bot briefings and answers update from that data → streak grows only on active days.
