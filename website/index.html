Fitness and nutrition

A personal trainer, a nutritionist, and the day you actually lived.

Elvora keeps workouts, meals, water, sleep, and coaching in one place. It stays quiet until you say hey elvora, and it will not invent a calorie, a step, or a night of sleep.

The only rule

If a number is on the screen, you put it there, or a sensor you connected did.

Estimates are labeled as estimates. A photo of a plate does not enter the diary until you confirm it. Uncertain voice logs wait the same way.

Product

Four parts of one day

Not a calorie counter with a few extra screens bolted on. The pieces share one record of you.

  1. 01

    Fitness

    Adaptive workouts, load that progresses, GPS for a walk or a run, and form demos that follow your equipment and how recovered you are.

  2. 02

    Nutrition

    Log by photo, voice, or text, then confirm before anything is saved. Macros, micronutrients, meal plans, and foods from where you live.

  3. 03

    Coach

    Voice and chat that read what you logged. Plans you can edit. Memory you can open, change, and delete.

  4. 04

    Health

    Readiness, sleep, water, streaks, and progress. A measurement and an estimate are not written the same way.

The day

Five places to work

The app stays small on purpose. Profile, devices, privacy, and subscriptions live under You.

  1. Home

    Daily command

    Readiness, calories against effort, macros, water, steps, sleep, and the next useful action.

  2. Train

    The session

    Plans for the gym, home, or outdoors. Logging, substitutions, and a GPS track when you go out.

  3. Nutrition

    The diary

    Meals, a plate scan, recipes, water, and suggestions from whatever is still left in the day’s targets.

  4. Coach

    The voice

    Wake Elvora, ask about the day, and get an answer that can point at your own records.

  5. Progress

    The record

    Measurements, photos, a weekly recap, and an export. Yours to keep or delete.

Coach

Useful, and not a clinic.

Listening

The coach connects workouts, meals, recovery, and goals. A suggestion shows where it came from. It does not diagnose, and it does not guess a calorie.

  • Listening after you say hey elvora
  • Thinking while it reads your logs
  • Speaking when the answer is ready
  • Uncertain photo and voice logs wait for you
  • Memory can be inspected, edited, and deleted
