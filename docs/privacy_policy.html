Who we are

NFL BOT (“we”) provides an AI health and fitness coaching app for wellness purposes (activity, training, nutrition, recovery).

Data we process

  • Account basics you enter (name, email, goals, body metrics)
  • Activity and fitness logs (steps, workouts, meals, water, sleep you log)
  • Optional device permissions: camera (meal photos), microphone (voice coach), activity recognition, Bluetooth (watch pairing), biometrics (optional lock)
  • Optional Health Connect / wearable readings when you connect them
  • Technical diagnostics needed to run and improve the app

How we use data

To provide coaching, personalize plans, sync across your devices, process subscriptions, and improve reliability. NFL BOT is not a medical device and does not provide medical diagnosis or treatment.

Sharing

We do not sell your personal health data. We may use processors (hosting, AI APIs, analytics, payment providers) under contracts that limit use to providing the service.

Retention & your controls

You can export or request deletion of account data from in-app Privacy settings or by contacting us. Some data may remain in backups for a limited period as required by law or security.

Children

NFL BOT is not directed at children under 13 (or the age required in your country).
